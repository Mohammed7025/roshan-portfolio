Projects

AI-Driven Smart Agriculture System

B.Tech major project. Project lead, team of 5. 2025 to 2026. Guide: Ms. Thasleena V A, Head of Department, IT.

Small and medium farmers often lack affordable soil testing and expert advice. We built a low-cost, end-to-end platform that reads live soil and weather data and returns crop, fertilizer and irrigation recommendations, with explanations a farmer can follow.

  • Hardware and data. A NodeMCU (ESP8266) reads NPK, moisture, pH, temperature and humidity sensors and syncs to Firebase in real time. Sensor readings, location-based weather and soil type feed the models.
  • Machine learning. Transformer-based models trained on public datasets recommend crops and fertilizers and decide when irrigation should be on or off. SHAP explanations show which factors drove each recommendation.
  • Software. A FastAPI backend with JWT authentication, a React web dashboard, a mobile app with a Malayalam language option, and AgroBot, a chatbot for crop, soil, irrigation and fertilizer questions.

Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, scikit-learn, FastAPI, React, Firebase, ESP8266, SHAP

AI Dietitian and Mental Health Tracker

B.Tech mini project. Project lead, team of 5. April 2025. Guide: Ms. Thasleena V A, Head of Department, IT.

A full-stack web platform that combines personalised diet planning with mental-wellness tracking.

  • Rule-based diet planner using BMI, dietary preferences and medical conditions, with a decision-tree fallback for edge cases.
  • Google Gemini API integration for a supportive chatbot and sentiment analysis of journal entries.
  • Daily mood, stress and sleep logging with interactive trend charts, and Firebase Authentication for sign-in.
  • Evaluated through user feedback.

Node.js, MongoDB Atlas, Firebase Authentication, JavaScript, HTML, CSS, Gemini API

Why a Master's, and why now

My projects showed me that the hardest and most valuable part of a useful system is the data and the models behind it. Building the smart agriculture platform meant training models on public datasets, explaining their output to non-experts with SHAP, and connecting them to real sensors. I want to move from applying these techniques to understanding them deeply, with stronger foundations in machine learning, explainability and data-driven systems.

Long-term goal: I aim to develop into an AI researcher and engineer who builds reliable, explainable, and socially useful intelligent systems. Through postgraduate study and research, I hope to strengthen my foundations in machine learning and data science, contribute to meaningful research, and eventually pursue doctoral study or research-oriented work.
